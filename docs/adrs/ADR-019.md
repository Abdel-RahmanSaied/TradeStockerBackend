# ADR-019: Separate local compatibility readiness from production decisions

Status: Accepted for local dependency planning only. Date: 2026-10-23.

Authority: owner's renewed instruction to continue local development through the plan; bounded split independently reviewed by independent-review-agent. This is not an identity, hosting, spending, risk, evaluation-threshold or live-activation approval.

## Observed problem

Original S00-T05 combines local Go/Python/PostgreSQL/NATS compatibility with D-05 identity, D-06 production deployment/budget and D-07 workload/recovery/cost targets. Its isolated technical subset was implemented and independently accepted at `1e778e4497fbcd2bb70cfc06cf9c820d5694395a`; the full task correctly remains BLOCKED. Requiring the full combined task before even building the S01-T01 module boundaries unnecessarily prevents authorized local work. S01-T01 has no decision_dependencies and does not select production identity or deployment.

## Precise dependency split

Add S00-T10 for the actual verified local compatibility prerequisite. Change only S01-T01's dependency from S00-T05 to S00-T10. Reuse the real implementation and independent evidence; do not claim it was newly written or create an invented test/reviewer. All S01-T01 acceptance criteria remain unchanged and must be independently met by its actual product entrypoints, package/build commands and CI.

Original S00-T05 retains its complete definition and blockers. S01-T04 (identity), S06-T04 (evaluation capacity) and S14-T01 (production platform) retain their full S00-T05 dependency and their own existing decision gates. All other dependencies and all original task acceptance criteria remain unchanged. D-01 through D-11 remain unresolved until their evidence/authority requirements are met. This split cannot activate a live lane or turn synthetic prices into a qualified feed.

## Evidence and limits

See [independent compatibility review](../evidence/compatibility-independent-review.md), [matrix](../evidence/compatibility-matrix.md) and [local profile](../evidence/load-and-cost-profile.md). The actual isolated run tested pinned client compatibility, simulated duplicate delivery with an inbox, and cross-process PostgreSQL checkpoint resume. It did not prove atomic producer-state/outbox commits, durable relay restart, sqlc/goose, S3/Parquet, production topology or identity. It did not migrate the product's Python interpreter. Those components remain governed by their original tasks and reviews.

## Consequence and verification

Local module/container/CI engineering may proceed on verified toolchain evidence while the production decisions are prepared for the owner. New dependencies and entrypoints still need their own tests, security review and exact commits. planctl DAG/state/generated-document checks verify the dependency edit's structural consistency; they do not prove product completion. No invariant or human-only execution boundary changes.
