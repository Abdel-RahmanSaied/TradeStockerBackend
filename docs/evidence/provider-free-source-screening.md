## Proposed route, not an approved vendor

First investigate a free Alpaca data entitlement for US historical research because its advertised depth is more useful than 100 daily observations or two years alone. This is conditional on lawful data-only account access, written entitlement scope and an actual explicit-feed sample. A Paper Only account must not be called consolidated-SIP eligible merely because a general FAQ describes delayed historical SIP. If the account only has IEX, record single-venue provenance and keep consolidated-dependent research disabled. Do not open or fund a live brokerage account to overcome this uncertainty.

The owner must perform any required signup and accept the actual provider/exchange agreement themselves. No account has been created, no agreement accepted, no subscription selected and no personal contact sent. The specifically approved SEC email must not be reused. Credentials must stay in protected local configuration, never chat, evidence or Git. Product configuration instructions must be implemented and reviewed before asking the owner to install keys under assumed variable names.

The first read-only validation must obtain and hash original responses, record request/feed/tier/venue/adjustment/timezone/retrieval and observation dates, follow pagination and rate limits, and test session gaps, duplicate/corrected bars, corporate actions and delisted/symbol-history coverage. Test a legitimately obtained sample against another permitted official reference where available. Record UNKNOWN fields, denied entitlements and publication delays rather than merging feeds or inventing fields. Historical daily bars alone do not establish current actionable freshness, quote/halt support or point-in-time fundamental availability.

The data adapter must allowlist only the approved data host and paths, redact keys, reject redirects that could leak them, bound retries/body sizes, and never include order or brokerage-account APIs. Keep real inputs separate from synthetic fixtures. No live lane, entry eligibility or backtest qualification changes follow from this screening.

S01 module/container/CI development and offline fixtures can continue while actual data access is unresolved. Frozen evaluation design, costs, holdouts, multiple-trial accounting and genuine elapsed prospective observation remain necessary before claiming mature recommendations; see [advisory readiness](first-advisory-readiness.md).
