## S00-T09 — Avoid unchanged paper-state rewrites while preserving expiry and financial safety

**Owner role:** Backend Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S00-T07`, `S00-T08`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-05`, `REQ-08`, `REQ-12`, `REQ-15`

### النطاق

Owner-authorized bounded performance fix arising from measured S00-T05 local compatibility work. Avoid rewriting an unchanged paper aggregate on reads, retaining transactional expiry, audit, risk, receipts and concurrent financial effects. Does not replace original S02 relational ledger tasks or resolve production decisions.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/store/`
- `docs/evidence/paper-read-performance.md`

### معايير القبول

- Unchanged authenticated paper reads and health checks do not rewrite the persisted financial document or updated_at; no stale in-memory cache replaces PostgreSQL authority.
- Due reservation expiry still releases funds and persists its journal and audit exactly once, including concurrent reads, retries and competing writes.
- Idempotency replay/conflict, rollback, risk policy, human-only lifecycle, validation and authentication semantics remain intact; no broker or live behavior is added.
- Compare the actual before/after local read workload at a fixed synthetic history size and record SQL write counts and latency without inventing production targets; preserve normal application data and accepted SEC research/frontend behavior.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Real PostgreSQL tests prove no update for unchanged reads, durable expiry with competing requests, rollback and idempotent/concurrent financial effects. Independently review exact commits; run race checks and a real-API browser paper workflow in an isolated environment.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.
