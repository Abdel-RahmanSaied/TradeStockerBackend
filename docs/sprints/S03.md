# S03 — بيانات السوق الأمريكي والمصري وجودتها

**Technical scope:** US and EGX ingestion and point-in-time data quality

> ملف مولّد؛ مصدر التعريف `../state/task_catalog.json`. الحالة الفعلية في `../state/project_state.json`، وليست في checkboxes هنا.

**اختياري:** لا؛ ضمن الخطة الأساسية.

## الهدف

Build independent adapters and prevent stale, invalid or unlicensed data from feeding actionable recommendations.

## بوابة الخروج

Synthetic adapters pass all gates; each real feed has separately recorded coverage, entitlements and validation evidence.

## S03-T01 — Define adapters and a synthetic/replay feed

**Owner role:** Go Data Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S02-T01`, `S02-T03`, `S02-T06`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-02`, `REQ-03`

### النطاق

Create a provider-neutral adapter interface for bars, quotes, trades, statuses and corporate actions. Declare capabilities explicitly rather than assuming identical feeds.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/providers/`
- `fixtures/marketdata/`

### معايير القبول

- Each capability specifies precision, timing, venue coverage and delay status.
- Synthetic feeds include late, out-of-order, duplicate and corrected records.
- Reconnect/backpressure policy is bounded and observable.
- Only one active subscription owner exists per provider partition.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Adapter conformance suite and subscription takeover/fencing tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.

## S03-T04 — Build normalization, quality scoring and freshness gates

**Owner role:** Go Data Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S03-T01`, `S02-T03`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-03`, `REQ-11`, `REQ-12`

### النطاق

Validate timestamps, clock drift, crossed/invalid quotes, duplicate trades, gaps and schema drift; expose quality reasons and per-strategy freshness requirements.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/dataquality/`
- `contracts/dataquality/`

### معايير القبول

- Use event, publication, receipt and decision timestamps distinctly.
- Unknown delay or stale critical data blocks new-entry qualification.
- Missing intervals are not forward-filled as traded volume.
- Data resumes only after bounded recovery checks; existing position monitoring continues in degraded mode.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Table-driven quality tests, stale-feed alarm and recovery hysteresis tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.

**Dependencies:** `S03-T01`, `S02-T06`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-03`, `REQ-04`, `REQ-09`

### النطاق

Backfill permitted history with resumable checkpoints and correction provenance; maintain raw prices, split-adjusted features and total-return series as distinct products.

### المخرجات المقترحة

### التحقق والاختبارات

## S03-T06 — Measure feed readiness per market and capability

**Owner role:** Data + QA Engineers · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S03-T01`, `S03-T04`, `S03-T05`

**Decisions to review:** `D-01`, `D-02`, `D-07`

**Requirements:** `REQ-02`, `REQ-03`, `REQ-13`

### النطاق

Create separate readiness reports for EGX and US and for EOD, intraday quotes/trades and optional L2. One blocked provider must not falsely qualify the other. Evaluate available feeds and record NO_GO for unavailable feeds; completion of this review never represents an unfinished adapter as implemented.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `docs/evidence/data-readiness-egx.md`
- `docs/evidence/data-readiness-us.md`

### معايير القبول

- Report measured gap rates, delay distribution, timestamp quality and coverage against preregistered thresholds.
- Maintain independent approval records; aggregate task completion is not live-lane activation.
- Perform source spot checks and document entitlement expiry.
- A missing provider leaves that market blocked, with independent-ready work continuing by approved task split if needed.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Run a complete sample trading session replay with injected outage.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.
