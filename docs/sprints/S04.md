## S04-T01 — Implement ledger and exact monetary arithmetic

**Owner role:** Go Domain Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S02-T02`, `S02-T04`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-08`, `REQ-10`

### النطاق

Implement an immutable balanced ledger per currency with explicit accounts for cash, settlement, fees, dividends and adjustments; separate security quantity subledger where appropriate.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/ledger/`
- `db/queries/ledger.sql`
- `tests/ledger/`

### معايير القبول

- Posted journals balance by currency; float conversion cannot silently change money.
- Corrections use reversing/replacement entries with causal links.
- An external event key cannot post twice.
- Rebuild derived balances from journal entries and compare to projections.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Property tests for balance/conservation; retry and rollback tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S04-T02 — Record human executions and partial fills

**Owner role:** Go Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S04-T01`, `S02-T01`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-06`, `REQ-08`

### النطاق

Accept actual fills reported by the owner or imported from statements. Treat recommendation acknowledgment, simulated fill and actual execution as different records.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/executions/`
- `internal/http/execution_reports/`

### معايير القبول

- Capture account, instrument, side, quantity, price, fees, currency, occurred time and provenance.
- Execution can be linked to a recommendation or independently entered; neither case sends an order.
- Partial fills, corrections and multiple fee lines preserve immutable accounting.
- Unauthorized and duplicate reports do not alter balances.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Concurrent duplicate submission, incorrect-account and partial-fill tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S04-T03 — Implement settlement, currency and buying-power views

**Owner role:** Go Domain Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S04-T02`, `S02-T03`

**Decisions to review:** `D-03`

**Requirements:** `REQ-02`, `REQ-08`, `REQ-10`

### النطاق

Derive settled/unsettled balances and broker-capability-aware cash availability. Maintain actual FX conversions separately from reporting currency marks.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/settlement/`
- `internal/fx/`
- `internal/portfolios/`

### معايير القبول

- No borrowing or unapproved cross-account cash pooling.
- Effective-dated rules determine when cash can fund the intended operation.
- FX source/time and conversion fees are visible; stale FX blocks affected personalized sizing.
- Deposits and withdrawals are not classified as trading profit.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Settlement holiday/DST, insufficient-cash, multicurrency and deposit attribution fixtures.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.

**Owner role:** Go + Data Engineers · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S04-T03`, `S03-T05`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-04`, `REQ-08`

### النطاق

## S04-T06 — Implement atomic capital reservations and race tests

**Owner role:** Go Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S04-T03`, `S02-T04`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-05`, `REQ-08`, `REQ-10`

### النطاق

Reserve advisory buying-power/risk budget for valid proposals to prevent mutually incompatible recommendations. Reservations are internal budgeting, never broker orders.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/allocations/`
- `tests/integration/capital_races/`

### معايير القبول

- Concurrent EGX/US or intraday/swing requests cannot allocate the same account cash twice.
- Expiry releases only the reservation; uncertain actual fills trigger reconciliation rather than assumed release.
- One account/instrument position owner strategy is enforced in the initial release.
- All advisory budgets remain bounded by confirmed actual balances.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- PostgreSQL parallel transaction tests for oversubscription, expiry and delayed fill reporting.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.
