## S07-T01 — Ingest official financial filings and preserve provenance

**Owner role:** Python Data Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S02-T06`, `S06-T01`

**Decisions to review:** `D-02`

**Requirements:** `REQ-03`, `REQ-04`

### النطاق

Implement SEC structured filings and contractually accessible EGX/issuer documents. Preserve original documents, publication timestamps, amendments and hashes.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `research/src/filings/`
- `contracts/financials/`

### معايير القبول

- Each filing records entity, period, publication time, accounting framework, currency and consolidation scope.
- Quarterly, annual and year-to-date contexts are not conflated.
- Amended filings remain linked to originals without rewriting earlier knowledge.
- HTML/XBRL/PDF extraction uses a safe pipeline; OCR is fallback only and is flagged.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- SEC fixture and permitted EGX document extraction tests with original-source references.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S07-T02 — Extract and reconcile financial facts

**Owner role:** Python Quant Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S07-T01`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-04`, `REQ-11`

### النطاق

Extract statements, notes and auditor findings into typed facts; derive ratios only from compatible contexts and verified arithmetic.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `research/src/financials/`
- `tests/financials/`

### معايير القبول

- Balance sheet, cash-flow and unit checks flag inconsistent extraction.
- Distinguish reported vs derived values and retain note/page/section references.
- Bank and nonfinancial-company templates avoid inappropriate identical ratios.
- Quality warnings, auditor issues and one-off gains are available to the committee.

### التحقق والاختبارات

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S07-T03 — Build financial-health and historical-behavior assessments

**Owner role:** Python Quant Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S07-T02`, `S06-T03`, `S03-T05`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-04`, `REQ-09`

### النطاق

Produce reusable financial health and stock-history reports including returns, volatility, drawdowns, liquidity and benchmark-relative behavior.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `research/src/assessments/`
- `contracts/assessments/`

### معايير القبول

- Use five years of financials and five-to-ten years of price history as targets when available, not invented minimum history.
- Short-history instruments expose limitations and pass only explicitly approved eligibility rules.
- Raw/adjusted series choices are documented for each metric.
- Assessments have validity conditions and explicit invalidation on material changes.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- New listing, missing quarters, split/dividend and restatement assessment tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.

**Dependencies:** `S02-T06`, `S03-T04`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-03`, `REQ-11`, `REQ-13`

### النطاق

Ingest official Fed/economic/central-bank sources and licensed news; deduplicate stories and normalize catalysts with event, expected, published and received times.

### المخرجات المقترحة

## S07-T05 — Implement evidence search and untrusted-content controls

**Owner role:** Python + Security Engineers · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S07-T01`, `S07-T04`, `S01-T06`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-04`, `REQ-11`, `REQ-12`, `REQ-13`

### النطاق

Build evidence retrieval using metadata/full-text first; add semantic retrieval only on a measured need. Treat source documents as data, not agent instructions.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `research/src/evidence/`
- `tests/security/retrieval/`

### معايير القبول

- Every material claim resolves to an accessible evidence ID and exact context.
- Unsupported claims and fabricated citations fail validation.
- Tool allowlists block code execution, secret access and arbitrary URL fetch from retrieved text.
- LLM-use rights and redaction are checked before sending documents to a provider.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Prompt-injection corpus, citation mismatch and forbidden-exfiltration tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S07-T06 — Evaluate extraction quality and mandatory evidence gates

**Owner role:** QA + Financial Reviewer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S07-T02`, `S07-T03`, `S07-T05`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-04`, `REQ-11`

### النطاق

Create a reviewed evaluation corpus for both markets with materiality-aware accuracy and missing-data behavior.

### المخرجات المقترحة

### التحقق والاختبارات
