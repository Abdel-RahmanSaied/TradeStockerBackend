## S08-T01 — Implement role contracts and conditional routing

**Owner role:** Python AI Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S07-T06`, `S02-T01`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-04`, `REQ-11`

### النطاق

Implement the eighteen research/control roles as logical modules, with five trading specialists activated later only when relevant. Do not launch every role on every tick.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `research/src/committee/roles/`
- `research/src/committee/router/`

### معايير القبول

- Role-to-data/capability requirements and cache invalidation are executable.
- Financial, historical, evidence and risk gates are mandatory for new entries.
- Logical role count is not represented as independent statistical evidence.
- Deterministic calculations remain outside LLM-generated arithmetic.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Route selection and missing-capability tests across all four lanes.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S08-T02 — Implement independent analysis, challenge and synthesis

**Owner role:** Python AI Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S08-T01`, `S07-T05`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-05`, `REQ-11`

### النطاق

Run independent first-pass assessments, bounded evidence-based challenge and synthesis. Preserve dissent and reasons to abstain.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `research/src/committee/graph/`
- `contracts/committee/`

### معايير القبول

- First-pass outputs are isolated from other roles’ conclusions.
- Debate has bounded rounds, tokens, concurrency and timeout.
- Chair cannot override deterministic veto or manufacture numerical success probabilities.
- Final assessment lists evidence, counterarguments, unknowns and invalidation conditions.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Conflicting-evidence, empty-consensus and majority-wrong fixtures.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S08-T03 — Add durable checkpoints and safe retries

**Owner role:** Python AI Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S08-T02`, `S02-T04`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-11`, `REQ-12`

### النطاق

Persist LangGraph state with a production checkpointer and versioned model/prompt/tool configuration. Reconcile uncertain tool outcomes before retrying consequential effects.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `research/src/committee/runtime/`
- `tests/committee/recovery/`

### معايير القبول

- Resume is tied to compatible workflow/config versions.
- Retries do not create duplicate reports or completed jobs.
- LLM-provider outage cannot bypass required research or stop deterministic position monitoring.
- No secrets, uncontrolled tools or broker capabilities enter graph state.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Crash/resume before and after model call and report persistence; incompatible-version recovery.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S08-T04 — Implement bounded caching, triggers and cost budgets

**Owner role:** Python + Go Engineers · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S08-T03`, `S05-T04`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-03`, `REQ-11`, `REQ-12`

### النطاق

Cache company/sector research by evidence/config hash and trigger refresh only when material inputs change. Isolate live versus batch quotas.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `research/src/committee/cache/`
- `internal/researchjobs/`

### معايير القبول

- TTL alone is insufficient when a material disclosure arrives; invalidate by event.
- Cache keys contain model/prompt/data/strategy versions and as-of time.
- Cost budget exhaustion yields defer/insufficient-data rather than silent weaker advice.
- Per-candidate token/time/cost records support operational analysis.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Material-news invalidation, budget exhaustion and concurrent refresh tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S08-T05 — Build committee evaluation and ablation suite

**Owner role:** Quant + AI QA · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S08-T02`, `S06-T04`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-09`, `REQ-11`

### النطاق

Compare committee-assisted strategies to deterministic baselines and reduced-role variants without contaminating holdouts.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `research/evals/committee/`
- `docs/evidence/committee-evaluation.md`

### معايير القبول

- Evaluate factuality, citation support, abstention, calibration where justified and incremental decision value.
- Historical LLM pretraining knowledge is treated as possible leakage; historical outputs are not called leakage-free.
- Forward shadow/paper evaluation is required for claims affected by model knowledge leakage.
- Do not tune prompts on the final holdout.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Run red-team, ablation, cost-per-accepted-assessment and stability evaluation.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S08-T06 — Integrate committee results through the Go decision gate

**Owner role:** Go + Python Engineers · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Requirements:** `REQ-04`, `REQ-05`, `REQ-06`, `REQ-11`

### النطاق

Consume versioned research assessments and finalize only through Go policy checks and fresh portfolio/data snapshots.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/researchresults/`
- `tests/integration/committee_core/`

### معايير القبول

- Malformed/unsubstantiated assessment is quarantined.
- Financial and historical inputs remain mandatory even for a high model score.
- Decision stores assessment version, policy version and fresh revalidation result.
- A completed graph alone cannot emit official actionable Telegram advice.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- End-to-end synthetic assessment-to-recommendation tests with veto and stale-input scenarios.
