## S09-T01 — Specify two versioned swing configurations

**Owner role:** Quant + Risk Reviewer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S06-T04`, `S08-T06`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-02`, `REQ-05`, `REQ-09`

### النطاق

Define entry, exit, time exit, catalyst, earnings exposure and liquidity logic separately for EGX_SWING and US_SWING.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `strategies/egx_swing/`
- `strategies/us_swing/`

### معايير القبول

- Rules are executable and have evidence-backed rationale, not a vague composite score.
- No forced minimum trade count or goal-driven risk increase.
- Benchmarks, costs, horizon and instrument eligibility are fixed before evaluation.
- Strategy config is immutable and approval-controlled.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Schema and boundary tests for both strategy configurations.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S09-T02 — Implement swing screening and candidate ranking

**Owner role:** Go + Python Engineers · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S09-T01`, `S03-T04`, `S07-T03`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-02`, `REQ-04`, `REQ-09`

### النطاق

Implement cheap deterministic screening followed by cached/deep research for qualifying candidates; rank opportunities after liquidity and cost constraints.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/signals/swing/`
- `research/src/strategies/swing/`

### معايير القبول

- Same input manifest/config produces stable candidate ordering.
- A high score cannot bypass missing financial evidence.
- Daily-bar finalization respects market calendar and late corrections.
- No recommendation is inferred from an incomplete current-session bar unless explicitly designed and tested.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Screening golden fixtures, repeated event and late-bar correction tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S09-T03 — Implement swing lifecycle and event-driven re-evaluation

**Owner role:** Go Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S09-T02`, `S05-T05`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-05`, `REQ-06`, `REQ-08`

### النطاق

Monitor actual positions for invalidation, time exit, earnings/catalysts and portfolio risk changes; generate human-action alerts.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/monitoring/swing/`
- `docs/runbooks/swing-monitoring.md`

### معايير القبول

- Exit alerts reference confirmed holdings and latest reliable data.
- Price/goal changes never automatically extend the strategy horizon.
- Stop and target prices are not represented as guaranteed fills.
- Monitoring continues if new entries are paused.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Gap, earnings, time-exit, stale-position and entry-pause tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.
