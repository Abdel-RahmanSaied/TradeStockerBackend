## S10-T01 — Register bot identities and secure incoming updates

**Owner role:** Go Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S01-T04`, `S05-T03`

**Decisions to review:** `D-10`

**Requirements:** `REQ-07`, `REQ-12`

### النطاق

Configure bot/webhook or approved polling mode, allowed user/chat identities and signed/opaque short-lived action references.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/telegram/`
- `docs/runbooks/telegram.md`

### معايير القبول

- Verify webhook secret header when using webhooks; deduplicate updates.
- Only paired authorized Telegram identities may access account information.
- Callback data never acts as authorization by itself.
- Bot token is redacted and rotatable with an incident runbook.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Forged webhook, replay, wrong-chat and expired-action tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S10-T02 — Implement notification outbox and ambiguous delivery handling

**Owner role:** Go Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S10-T01`, `S02-T04`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-07`, `REQ-12`

### النطاق

Persist delivery attempts and Telegram message identifiers. Distinguish unique logical recommendations from possibly duplicated external messages after timeout.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/notifications/`
- `db/queries/delivery.sql`

### معايير القبول

- Use PENDING/SENDING/SENT/UNKNOWN/FAILED states with bounded retries and rate-limit handling.
- Do not claim end-to-end exactly-once delivery; timeout after send may be ambiguous.
- Expose recommendation ID/revision in each message to identify repeats.
- Expired entries are not resent as fresh actionable advice.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Timeout before/after remote acceptance, retry-after and process-crash tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.

**Dependencies:** `S10-T02`, `S08-T06`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-06`, `REQ-07`

### النطاق

### المخرجات المقترحة

### التحقق والاختبارات

## S10-T04 — Implement human availability and actual-fill workflow

**Owner role:** Go Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S10-T03`, `S04-T02`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-06`, `REQ-07`, `REQ-08`

### النطاق

Provide acknowledge, ignore, watch and record-execution interactions; collect actual fill details and revalidate context without submitting an order.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/telegram/actions/`
- `internal/availability/`

### معايير القبول

- Record execution requires price, quantity, account and actual timestamp.
- Acknowledgment is not a fill and does not debit the ledger.
- User-unavailable mode suppresses time-critical new entries but not risk monitoring.
- Late or partial execution triggers reconciliation and risk refresh.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Full message-to-manual-fill and wrong-account scenarios; prove no order network call occurs.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S10-T05 — Operate shadow/paper runs and collect latency observations

**Owner role:** QA + Owner · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S10-T04`, `S06-T06`, `S09-T06`

**Decisions to review:** `D-10`

**Requirements:** `REQ-07`, `REQ-09`, `REQ-14`

### النطاق

Run controlled shadow and paper sessions with paired Telegram account; measure delivery and human response separately.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `docs/evidence/swing-shadow-paper.md`

### معايير القبول

- Actual and assumed fills remain separate in UI and reports.
- Unknown delivery is visible rather than treated as confirmed reading.
- Collect received/read-response/manual-fill times only where actually observable.
- Forward-run evidence includes outages, skipped alerts and user absence.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Session rehearsal with disconnected bot, unavailable user and provider outage.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.
