## S11-T03 — Define human-feasible EGX/US intraday strategies

**Owner role:** Quant + Risk Reviewer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S11-T02`, `S10-T05`, `S05-T06`

**Decisions to review:** `D-03`, `D-04`

**Requirements:** `REQ-02`, `REQ-05`, `REQ-06`, `REQ-07`

### النطاق

Define two separate intraday strategy IDs and entry ranges long enough for measured human action. Add cooldown, max entries, no-revenge-trading and session-cutoff policies.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `strategies/egx_intraday/`
- `strategies/us_intraday/`

### معايير القبول

- No scalping or immediate execution assumption.
- Same-session eligibility/cash rules are checked per account and instrument.
- Unavailability and excessive delivery/user delay block fresh actionable entries.
- Intraday loss cannot be relabeled as swing exposure without a separately approved policy; unexpected overnight exposure raises a review.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Measured-latency sensitivity, cooldown, deadline and ineligible-security tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.
