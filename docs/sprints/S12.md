## S12-T01 — Implement versioned goal definitions and permissions

**Owner role:** Go Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S04-T05`, `S05-T01`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-05`, `REQ-10`

### النطاق

Define capital, base currency, target metric, horizon, deposits/withdrawals, risk caps and user availability with explicit approval and revision history.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/goals/`
- `contracts/goals/`

### معايير القبول

- Target, forecast and risk budget are separate fields.
- Goals cannot change risk policy or activate a strategy.
- Unknown target feasibility is a valid state.
- No historical example capital amount or profit rate becomes an assumed user choice.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Auth, optimistic concurrency and attempted risk-escalation tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S12-T02 — Implement feasibility and scenario analysis

**Owner role:** Python Quant Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S12-T01`, `S06-T04`, `S06-T06`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-09`, `REQ-10`

### النطاق

Evaluate goals using approved out-of-sample evidence, costs and correlated return paths; support insufficient-evidence and conflicts-with-risk outcomes.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `research/src/goals/`
- `research/evals/goals/`

### معايير القبول

- Report assumptions and uncertainty; no model-invented success percentage.
- Preserve loss clustering/cross-asset dependencies in scenario choices or disclose limitations.
- Do not infer guaranteed attainability from backtest mean returns.
- No recommendation to borrow or automatically increase risk.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Hand-check required-return arithmetic and extreme/cost/deposit scenarios.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S12-T03 — Implement conservative capital allocation proposals

**Owner role:** Go + Quant Engineers · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S12-T02`, `S04-T06`, `S11-T06`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-05`, `REQ-08`, `REQ-10`

### النطاق

Propose lane budgets within current risk limits and confirmed balances; keep cash idle when suitable opportunities do not exist.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/goals/allocation/`
- `tests/goals/invariants/`

### معايير القبول

- All allocations pass the same policy kernel and account-level atomic reservation.
- Goal deadlines cannot multiply loss limits or alter strategies.
- Cross-currency transfers require actual recorded funding, not hypothetical available value.
- Policy changes require explicit human-reviewed revisions.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Aggressive-target, insufficient-cash and correlated-lane allocation tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S12-T04 — Implement progress, drawdown and cost attribution

**Owner role:** Go + Quant Engineers · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S12-T01`, `S04-T05`, `S06-T03`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-08`, `REQ-09`, `REQ-10`

### النطاق

Report realized/unrealized returns, FX contribution, trading costs, operating costs and cash flows separately; measure horizon-end attainment as well as temporary crossing.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/goals/progress/`
- `research/src/performance/attribution/`

### معايير القبول

- Deposits are not profits; withdrawals do not automatically become losses.
- Actual and paper goal progress use isolated balances.
- Currency source/time and stale valuation are visible.
- Target attainment does not trigger a broker sell order.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Cash-flow, FX, operating-cost and realized-versus-unrealized fixtures.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S12-T05 — Deliver goal reviews and safe target-revision workflow

**Owner role:** Go + Product Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S12-T02`, `S12-T04`, `S10-T03`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-07`, `REQ-10`

### النطاق

Provide scheduled or event-driven goal status and proposed changes to goal/term, never automatic increases in risk.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `internal/notifications/goals/`
- `internal/goals/revisions/`

### معايير القبول

- Every forecast links to an assessment version and expiry.
- Low-feasibility messages explain no-trade or revised horizon options without guarantees.
- Goal changes require authenticated explicit confirmation and append history.
- No loss-chasing, averaging down or horizon switch is introduced implicitly.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Telegram goal message, revision audit and unauthorized change tests.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.


## S12-T06 — Evaluate goal policy against no-goal baseline

**Owner role:** Quant + Risk Reviewer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S12-T03`, `S12-T04`, `S12-T05`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-05`, `REQ-09`, `REQ-10`

### النطاق

Test whether the goal layer changes risk, drawdowns, turnover and net outcomes compared with the same strategies without goals.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `docs/evidence/goal-layer-evaluation.md`

### معايير القبول

- Report shortfall, attainment at horizon, drawdown breaches and costs.
- Maintain preregistered evaluation and avoid selecting only successful start dates.
- Reject goal logic that breaches policy even when it increases returns.
- Reviewer signs an evidence report without promising future performance.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Historical and forward-paper goal scenarios with withheld periods.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.
