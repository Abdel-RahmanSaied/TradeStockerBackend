**Owner role:** Frontend Engineer · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-04`, `REQ-07`, `REQ-11`, `REQ-13`

### النطاق

Expose recommendation revisions, committee dissent, original evidence references, data quality and deterministic veto reasons.

### المخرجات المقترحة

**Dependencies:** `S13-T04`, `S05-T05`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-05`, `REQ-12`

### النطاق
