**Technical scope:** Independent lane activation and post-release evaluation

> ملف مولّد؛ مصدر التعريف `../state/task_catalog.json`. الحالة الفعلية في `../state/project_state.json`، وليست في checkboxes هنا.

**اختياري:** لا؛ ضمن الخطة الأساسية.

## الهدف

Move each market/strategy through forward-paper and human-reviewed limited advisory gates, never from a calendar deadline.

## بوابة الخروج

Each lane has a recorded GO or NO_GO; actual execution remains human-only; continuing evaluation and disable procedures are operational.

**Requirements:** `REQ-02`, `REQ-09`, `REQ-14`

## S15-T05 — Operate staged advisory and outcome attribution

**Owner role:** Owner + Operations + Quant · **Priority:** P0 · **Relative size:** M

**Dependencies:** `S14-T06`, `S10-T06`, `S11-T06`, `S12-T06`

**Decisions to review:** No additional decision ID.

**Requirements:** `REQ-06`, `REQ-08`, `REQ-09`, `REQ-14`

### النطاق

Record per-lane GO/NO_GO outcomes, run only approved lanes with limited scope and compare actual executions to advice and paper assumptions.

### المخرجات المقترحة

- `docs/evidence/limited-advisory-operations.md`

### معايير القبول

- A NO_GO verdict is a valid completed review task, not an activated lane.
- Live enablement requires explicit external owner authorization not just a task status.
- Record skipped trades, user deviations, rejected candidates and uncertain fills.
- Drift or safety breach pauses entries and triggers reviewed investigation.
- Start only a lane whose own S15 review has completed with GO and whose external owner approval is recorded; no other lane is implicitly enabled.

### التحقق والاختبارات

- Live-advisory dry-run checklist and simulated pause/rollback of one lane.

**Evidence:** أضف دليلًا فعليًا داخل `docs/evidence/` واربطه بالحالة. لا تعني هذه القائمة أن الملفات أو الاختبارات موجودة الآن.
